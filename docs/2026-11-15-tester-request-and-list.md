# Tester request and list, Week 1 task 5

Date: 2026-11-15. Requests go out Dec 7. Sessions happen Feb 22 to Mar 14, after the checkpoint.

## Who qualifies

Target: 3 to 5 real marketers who say yes. Message 10 to get there.

A person qualifies if they run email or lifecycle marketing for a small ecommerce brand, alone or with one other person, and send with a tool like Klaviyo or Mailchimp. That is the core user from Part A.

A freelance retention consultant or small agency marketer also qualifies. The plan says to recruit from this group because one consultant brings several brands. This is INFERRED in Part A, not tested.

Does not qualify: friends who are not marketers, classmates who have never run an email program, and anyone at a large company with a data team. Their feedback is real, but it is not the evidence the case study needs.

## The message

Keep it short. Send it one person at a time, never as a mass message. Fill the brackets by hand.

Subject, if email: Short favor, 30 minutes in late February

Hi [first name],

I am an MBA marketing student at Arkansas State, and I am building a small free tool that keeps a ledger of email experiments. You record the bet before you send, it works out the lift against a holdout, and it says plainly when the result is too small to tell.

It is not ready yet. I am asking early so I can plan. Would you be open to trying it in late February or early March? It would be one session of about 30 minutes where you use it on sample data, not your own customers. I would ask a few questions and write down your answers as you say them.

I am asking you because [one true sentence: what they run, or what you saw them post or say about email testing].

If you are not the right person, I would be glad to hear who is. No problem at all if the timing does not work.

Geetansh

## Rules for using it

- Replace the "because" sentence with something true about that person. If you have nothing true to say, do not send that one.
- Do not attach screenshots or claim the tool does more than it does today. As of this date it is a schema, a redaction module and a plan.
- The 30 minutes is my guess, not from the plan. Change it if you want a longer session.
- Sample data only. No tester uploads real customer data in these sessions. Updated 2026-11-18: this still holds for the 30 minute sessions. The owner approved a separate real data experiment, run by the tester on their own data under written consent, described in `2026-11-18-pilot-kit.md`.
- Quotes from a tester appear in the case study only with their written consent, and only after redaction.
- If fewer than 3 testers are recruited by Feb 28, the case study says so.

## What the session asks, from the validation plan

Record every answer as written.

Ask only the questions whose feature exists on the session date, using `docs/2026-11-17-build-status.md`. On 2026-11-17 that is questions 1, 2 and 7. Questions 3 and 5 need the journey and pipeline views, question 4 needs the evidence chain, and question 6 needs the pain ranking. None of those is built, so asking them would test a feature the tester cannot see. Added 2026-11-17, review finding R4-4.

1. Describe the look and feel in one word.
2. Did it feel different from other marketing tools you use.
3. Did the journey and pipeline views show you something you did not already know, and what.
4. Same question for the theme to revenue evidence chain.
5. Find the step where most people leave. Record found or not found, and seconds taken.
6. Did the pain ranking match your view, and which pain did you underrate or miss.
7. Was an inconclusive result useful to you.

The success metric below needs the journey and pipeline views, which are not built. If they are not built by the session date, the metric cannot be measured, and the case study must say so instead of replacing it with a different metric after the fact.

Success metric, set now and not changed after seeing results: at least 3 of 5 testers find the step where the most people leave, in the demo, within 3 minutes, without help. The 3 of 5 and 3 minutes thresholds are judgment, not a benchmark.
