How the results should be used

  • A result is an estimate with its uncertainty shown. It is not proof, and it is not advice.
  • A result that says inconclusive does not mean there was no effect. It means the data could not tell.
  • Choosing how to read a result after seeing the data weakens it. The ledger records the plan before the send so you can tell the difference.
