Email check

Check a marketing email against US email law and the Gmail and Yahoo sender rules before it goes out. Each rule shows its source and the date it was checked, 2026-11-19. Sending is not approved while a rule fails or a statement only you can make is not ticked. Nothing you type here is stored or sent anywhere.

This is not legal advice. It covers US commercial email only. GDPR and CASL checks are not built. To launch a ledger entry, run this check on the entry’s own page, where a pass is recorded in its history.
