Customer Insight Loom

Customer journeys, pipelines and email experiments in one picture, with the evidence kept and the uncertainty shown. When a result is too small to tell, it says so plainly.

Experiments

Write the bet down before you send, and read the result once.

  • Ledger

    Record a hypothesis, check the audience is large enough, hold back a control group and read out the result.

  • Email check

    Check an email against US email law and the Gmail and Yahoo sender rules, with the source of each rule.

  • Email drafts

    Draft an email for a segment and a complaint in PAS, FAB or AIDA, then approve it in a sandbox.

Customers

Where people drop off, where deals wait, and what customers say about it.

  • Journeys

    Linked charts of 100,000 synthetic customers, the designed journey and who is behind each step.

  • Pipeline

    How long deals sit in each stage, which have stalled, and the pipeline on any past date.

  • Comments

    Themes in customer comments, with names removed, and the evidence chain from a complaint to revenue.

Your data

Bring files in, check them, and see what this tab holds.

  • Data intake

    Load a CSV and map its columns. Columns that look like names, emails or phone numbers are blocked.

  • Data check

    Match two files on email and check a time column against clock changes.

  • Webhooks

    Try the receiver for events from other tools, with signing, limits and step names to map.

  • Your data

    What this tab holds, how much memory it uses, and a control to delete everything.

Where your data goes

Your files stay in your browser. There is no account, and nothing you load is sent to a server. See Privacy for the details.

This is an early version. It has not been tried on real customer data, and names are only partly removed from text. The Ledger has demo data that shows the whole path, and Methods lists the checks run on the statistics.
