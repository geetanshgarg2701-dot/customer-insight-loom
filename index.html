Customer Insight Loom

Customer journeys, pipelines and email experiments, woven into one picture with the evidence kept and the uncertainty shown. It is free. The ledger records each experiment: you write down the bet before you send, hold back a control group, and read the result once, after the conversion window closes. When a result is too small to tell, it says so plainly.

Your files stay in your browser. There is no account, and nothing you load is sent to a server. See Privacy for the details.

What works today

  • Ledger. Write down a hypothesis, check that your audience is large enough, assign a holdout, and read out the result. The demo data on that page shows the whole path.
  • Data intake. Load a CSV, map its columns, and preview 20 rows. Columns that look like names, emails or phone numbers are blocked.
  • Journeys. Linked charts of where customers drop off, on synthetic data.
  • Pipeline. Where deals wait, how long they sit in each stage, and which ones have stalled, on synthetic data.
  • Data check. Match two files on email and check a time column against clock changes, in the browser.
  • Methods. The checks that ran on generated data to test the calculator, with the counts.

This is an early version. It has not been tried on real customer data, and names are not removed from text yet. Use the demo data to see how it works.
