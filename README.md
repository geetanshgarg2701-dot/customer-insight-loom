# Customer Insight Loom

Customer journeys, pipelines and email experiments, woven into one picture with the evidence kept and the uncertainty shown. It is free and runs entirely in your browser.

**Live app: https://geetanshgarg2701-dot.github.io/customer-insight-loom/**

- **Journeys.** Linked charts of where customers drop off, a flow with drop off, top paths, a what if, and a view of what customers did before and after any one event.
- **Pipeline.** Where deals wait, how long they sit in each stage, which have stalled, a period comparison and an outlook for open deals.
- **Ledger.** Record the bet before you send, assign a holdout, and read the result with its uncertainty. A result stays locked until the conversion window closes, and it says plainly when a test is too small to tell.
- **Data check.** Match two files on email, and check a time column against clock changes.
- **Methods.** The checks that ran on generated data, with the counts.

Your files are read in the browser and are not uploaded anywhere. Close the page and they are gone. The charts and the demo use synthetic data. It has not been tried on real customer data, results are estimates and not advice, and it does not remove personal names from text.
